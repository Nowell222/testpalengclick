1. System Scope & Core Modules

Paleng-Click Connect is designed specifically for Philippine local public markets (palengke), in full compliance with the DILG-BSP Paleng-QR Ph Plus mandate:

  • Market Administrator Portal: Section and stall mapping, multi-year sqm rental rate matrix (2026–2028), automated 25% late payment penalties, and real-time revenue collection KPI dashboards.
  • Offline-Ready Cashiering (PWA): Allows market cashiers and field collectors to record collections and print PDF/thermal receipts with IndexedDB offline syncing even during market blackouts or cellular dead zones.
  • Vendor Self-Service Portal: Allows market vendors to check real-time Statement of Account (SOA), historical arrears, and settle stall fees online.
  • Integrated Digital Payment Gateway: Seamless integration with PayMongo or Xendit supporting QR Ph, GCash, Maya, and online bank transfers with automated remittance to the LGU's Landbank / DBP account.
  • Smart AI Receipt Auditor: Built-in OCR scanner (Google Gemini + Tesseract) to automatically parse bank reference numbers, timestamps, and amounts for proof-of-payment verification.
  • BPLO & Regulatory Clearance Module: Manage business permit clearances, sanitary inspection status, and transient (bolante) vendor tickets.
